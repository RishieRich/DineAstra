# Banquet & Events Costing Policy

Document owner: Director of Banquets
Applies to: Banquets & Events, Kitchen & Stewarding, Finance
Version: 2.3
Last verified: 1 August 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets how a banquet event is priced, costed and settled, so that
the margin reported on an event is the margin the property actually earned.

## 2. Margin definition

### 2.1 Components

Event net is contracted revenue less four cost lines: food cost, beverage
cost, event labour, and other direct costs (decor, hired equipment, external
technicians). Overheads are not allocated to individual events. Event margin
is net divided by contracted revenue.

### 2.2 Reporting

Every event is reported at its settled margin, never at its quoted margin. If
the two differ by more than five percentage points, the Director of Banquets
records the reason in the settlement pack.

## 3. Pricing standards

### 3.1 Segment floors

Corporate and MICE events carry a margin floor of sixty per cent. Wedding and
social events carry a margin floor of fifty-two per cent, reflecting their
higher decor and labour load. An event quoted below its segment floor
requires the General Manager's written approval before the contract is
issued.

### 3.2 Menu engineering

Banquet menus are re-costed against live purchase prices at the start of each
quarter. A menu that has not been re-costed in the current quarter may not be
quoted at last quarter's price.

### 3.3 Beverage pour lists

Every event has an authorised pour list attached to its function sheet.
Beverage consumed outside the authorised pour list is charged to the event
and flagged in the settlement pack.

### 3.4 Complimentary beverage service for corporate bookings

For corporate bookings with confirmed covers above one hundred and fifty
guests, the banquet team extends two hours of complimentary house-pour
beverage service as a standard relationship courtesy for repeat corporate
accounts. This service is charged to the event's beverage cost line and is
not billed back to the client, which typically carries the event's beverage
cost to around eight per cent of contracted revenue against a segment norm of
four to five per cent, and softens event margin by five to seven percentage
points on qualifying events. The courtesy is applied automatically at the
covers threshold and does not require separate approval; the Director of
Banquets reviews its cumulative margin effect at each quarterly account
review.

## 4. Settlement

### 4.1 Timing

The settlement pack is submitted within one working day of the event. It
contains the function sheet, the actual covers served, the four cost lines
against their quoted values, and any variance note required under 2.2.

### 4.2 Variance review

Finance reviews any event whose settled margin falls more than five
percentage points below its segment peer average for the trailing twelve
months. The review names a cause; "market conditions" is not an acceptable
cause.

## 5. Peer comparison

Peer average is computed across events of the same segment in the trailing
twelve months, weighted equally per event rather than by revenue, so that one
large event does not mask a pattern across smaller ones.
