# Purchase Requisition & Expense Policy

Document owner: Financial Controller
Applies to: All departments
Version: 1.8
Last verified: 20 July 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs how the property raises, approves and reviews purchase
requisitions, and how cost variance between requisitions is caught before
payment rather than after.

## 2. Raising a requisition

### 2.1 Required fields

Every requisition carries the item, the department raising it, the vendor, the
unit rate, the quantity, and the name of the person raising it. A requisition
missing any of these fields is returned unapproved.

### 2.2 Vendor selection

Departments buy from the approved vendor list. Where a department buys the
same item from a vendor outside that list, the requisition must carry a
written reason naming why the contracted vendor was not used.

## 3. Approval thresholds

### 3.1 Departmental limit

A department head may approve a requisition up to twenty-five thousand rupees
in a single line. Above that limit the requisition requires the Financial
Controller's approval, and above one lakh rupees it requires the General
Manager's.

### 3.2 Splitting

Splitting a purchase across multiple requisitions to stay below an approval
threshold is a disciplinary matter, not an administrative one.

## 4. Cost variance review

### 4.1 Same-item variance rule

Where two or more requisitions for the same item are raised on the same
trading day, the Financial Controller compares their unit rates. A unit-rate
variance greater than forty per cent between the lowest and highest
requisition for that item must be held for General Manager review before
either requisition is paid. Both requisitions are held, not only the more
expensive one, because the cheaper line is sometimes the one carrying a
quality or quantity substitution.

### 4.2 Cause classification

A held variance is closed with one of four causes: contracted rate change,
vendor substitution, specification difference, or error. A variance closed as
"error" triggers a review of the raising department's last thirty days of
requisitions for the same item.

### 4.3 Cross-department purchasing

Where two departments buy the same item on the same day from different
vendors, the Financial Controller consolidates future purchasing of that item
under the department holding the contracted rate, unless a specification
difference is recorded under 4.2.

## 5. Payment

No requisition held under section 4 is released for payment until its cause
is recorded. A held requisition that is paid without a recorded cause is
reported to the owner in the monthly pack.
