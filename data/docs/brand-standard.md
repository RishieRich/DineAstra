# DineAstra Operating Standard

Document owner: General Manager
Applies to: All departments
Version: 4.1
Last verified: 11 September 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This brand standard defines the minimum operating quality expected across
all four outlets on every trading day. It is not an aspiration document. Each clause
is written so that a department head can answer "did we do this, yes or no"
without interpretation.

## 2. Guest promise

The group promises three things and is measured on them: a table ready when
it was promised, food served at the temperature it was cooked, and a bill that
matches what the guest ordered. Everything in this document exists to protect
one of those three promises, whether the guest is seated in a dining room or
opening a delivery bag at home.

## 3. Governance

### 3.1 Ownership

Every clause in this document has a named owning department. Where two
departments share a clause, the department listed first owns the sign-off and
the second supports it.

### 3.2 Review cycle

This document is reviewed on the eleventh of each month by the General
Manager with the six department heads. The "last verified" date at the head
of this document is updated at that review and nowhere else. A clause that
has not been verified in ninety days is treated as unverified and must not be
quoted to a guest, an auditor, or an owner as current practice.

## 4. Daily departmental standards

### 4.1 Scope

Section 4 covers the work that must happen in every outlet on every trading
day, regardless of cover count, event load, or season. Event-specific
standards are held separately in the Banquet and Events Costing Policy.

### 4.2 Daily task checklist

Every outlet begins and closes each trading day against a fixed departmental
checklist of thirty-four items. Department heads sign off each item in the daily log before
handover; an unsigned item is treated as not done regardless of whether the
work occurred, because guest experience is protected by verification, not
assumption.

**Kitchen**

1. Record walk-in, chiller and deep-freeze temperatures at 07:00, 15:00 and 23:00.
2. Check every incoming delivery against the purchase requisition before accepting it.
3. Label and date every prepared item held for next-day service.
4. Discard and log any item held beyond its labelled shelf life.
5. Taste and sign off the day's sauces and stocks before service opens.
6. Complete the daily food-cost entry against the outlet's sales report.
7. Close the kitchen with a signed sanitation walk of all preparation areas.

**Service & Floor**

8. Complete the outlet opening walk before the first cover is seated.
9. Verify that menu pricing on the POS matches the printed menu.
10. Reset and check every table setting between covers.
11. Log the reason for any comped or voided item at the time it is raised.
12. Reconcile covers against POS entries at the close of each meal period.
13. Close the floor with a written record of any guest complaint and its resolution.

**Bar & Beverage**

14. Count opening stock against the previous night's closing sheet.
15. Check draught lines and gas pressure before service opens.
16. Verify that every open bottle carries its opening date.
17. Log every spillage and breakage as it happens, not at close.
18. Close the bar with a signed stock count and a variance note.

**Host & Reservations**

19. Confirm the day's bookings and note every large party before 11:00.
20. Check that every held table is released if the party has not arrived within fifteen minutes.
21. Record every walk-in turned away, with the time and the reason.
22. Confirm the next day's private-dining bookings with the client contact by 18:00.
23. Hand over the repeat-guest and allergy list to the duty manager at shift close.

**Delivery & Packaging**

24. Check packaging stock against the day's forecast before the first order.
25. Verify that every aggregator menu matches the current in-house menu and price.
26. Temperature-check one dispatched order per hour and log the reading.
27. Log every order rejected, cancelled or refunded, with the reason.
28. Reconcile aggregator order counts against the POS at close.

**Facilities & Safety**

29. Run the morning plant check and log refrigeration and exhaust readings.
30. Test the fire-suppression panel on the published weekly rotation.
31. Confirm that all fire exits and walkways are clear before service opens.
32. Clear the guest-facing maintenance queue within the same trading day.
33. Verify pest-control station checks against the contracted schedule.
34. Close the day with a written log of every open fault and its owner.

### 4.3 Escalation

Any checklist item that cannot be completed must be escalated to the duty
manager before shift close with a named reason and a committed completion
time. Silent non-completion is treated as a brand-standard breach.

## 5. Measurement

Compliance is measured as the percentage of the thirty-four checklist items
signed off across all six departments on a given trading day. The group target
is one hundred per cent; the owner reporting threshold is ninety-five per
cent.

## 6. Exceptions

Only the General Manager may waive a clause in this document, in writing, for
a named date and a named reason. A verbal waiver is not a waiver.
