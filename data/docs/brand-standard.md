# Darpan Brand Standard

Document owner: General Manager
Applies to: All departments
Version: 4.1
Last verified: 11 September 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This brand standard defines the minimum operating quality expected at the
property on every trading day. It is not an aspiration document. Each clause
is written so that a department head can answer "did we do this, yes or no"
without interpretation.

## 2. Guest promise

The property promises three things and is measured on them: a clean room
ready when promised, food served at the temperature it was cooked, and a bill
that matches what the guest agreed to. Everything in this document exists to
protect one of those three promises.

## 3. Governance

### 3.1 Ownership

Every clause in this document has a named owning department. Where two
departments share a clause, the department listed first owns the sign-off and
the second supports it.

### 3.2 Review cycle

This document is reviewed on the eleventh of each month by the General
Manager with the six department heads. The "last verified" date at the head
of this document is updated at that review and nowhere else. A clause that
has not been verified in ninety days is treated as unverified and must not be
quoted to a guest, an auditor, or an owner as current practice.

## 4. Daily departmental standards

### 4.1 Scope

Section 4 covers the work that must happen every trading day regardless of
occupancy, event load, or season. Seasonal and event-specific standards are
held separately in the Banquet and Events Costing Policy.

### 4.2 Daily task checklist

Every outlet begins and closes each trading day against a fixed departmental
checklist. Department heads sign off each item in the daily log before
handover; an unsigned item is treated as not done regardless of whether the
work occurred, because guest experience is protected by verification, not
assumption.

**Housekeeping**

1. Verify overnight room-status report against the PMS before 07:00.
2. Inspect every departure room before it is released for sale.
3. Deep-clean the public restrooms on the lobby level twice per shift.
4. Replenish amenity kits in all occupied rooms by 14:00.
5. Log and escalate any linen shortfall to the Executive Housekeeper.
6. Walk the corridors on every floor and clear service trays within 30 minutes.
7. Close the day with a written handover naming every out-of-order room.

**Front Office**

8. Reconcile the night audit before the morning shift begins.
9. Confirm every arrival with a banquet or event linkage by 10:00.
10. Check that all posted rates match the contracted rate for the segment.
11. Clear the unassigned-room queue before 16:00.
12. Verify that every in-house guest folio has no unposted charges at 22:00.
13. Hand over the VIP and repeat-guest list to the duty manager at shift close.

**Food & Beverage Service**

14. Complete the outlet opening checklist before the first cover is seated.
15. Taste and log the temperature of every buffet item at service start.
16. Verify that the day's menu pricing on the POS matches the printed menu.
17. Refresh table linen between every cover in the all-day dining room.
18. Reconcile outlet covers against POS entries at the close of each meal period.
19. Close the outlet with a written record of any guest complaint and its resolution.

**Kitchen & Stewarding**

20. Record walk-in and deep-freeze temperatures at 06:00, 14:00 and 22:00.
21. Check every incoming delivery against the purchase requisition before acceptance.
22. Label and date every prepared item held for next-day service.
23. Discard and log any item held beyond its labelled shelf life.
24. Complete the daily food-cost entry against the outlet sales report.
25. Close the kitchen with a signed sanitation walk of all preparation areas.

**Banquets & Events**

26. Confirm the next day's function sheet with the client contact by 18:00.
27. Walk every event space against the function sheet before guest arrival.
28. Verify the beverage opening stock against the event's authorised pour list.
29. Record the actual covers served against the contracted covers.
30. Submit the event settlement pack within one working day of the event.

**Engineering & Maintenance**

31. Run the morning plant-room check and log generator and boiler readings.
32. Clear the guest-facing maintenance queue within the same trading day.
33. Test emergency lighting and fire panels on the published weekly rotation.
34. Close the day with a written log of every open fault and its owner.

### 4.3 Escalation

Any checklist item that cannot be completed must be escalated to the duty
manager before shift close with a named reason and a committed completion
time. Silent non-completion is treated as a brand-standard breach.

## 5. Measurement

Compliance is measured as the percentage of checklist items signed off across
all six departments on a given trading day. The property target is one
hundred per cent; the owner reporting threshold is ninety-five per cent.

## 6. Exceptions

Only the General Manager may waive a clause in this document, in writing, for
a named date and a named reason. A verbal waiver is not a waiver.
